
Q: What is IRCTC?
A: IRCTC is the official online platform for booking Indian Railway tickets.

Q: How can I create an IRCTC account?
A: You can register on the IRCTC website or mobile app using your email and mobile number.

Q: How do I log in to IRCTC?
A: Enter your username and password on the login page.

Q: How can I book a train ticket?
A: Select source, destination, date, choose a train, and complete payment.

Q: Can I book tickets without login?
A: No, login is required to book tickets.

Q: What is Tatkal booking?
A: Tatkal is a scheme for last-minute ticket booking.

Q: What is Tatkal booking time?
A: AC at 10 AM and non-AC at 11 AM.

Q: What is Premium Tatkal?
A: It is a dynamic pricing Tatkal scheme with higher charges.

Q: What is PNR number?
A: PNR is a unique number for your booked ticket.

Q: How to check PNR status?
A: Enter your PNR number on the IRCTC website or app.

Q: What does RAC mean?
A: Reservation Against Cancellation allows partial travel.

Q: What is waitlist?
A: It means your ticket is not yet confirmed.

Q: Can waitlist tickets get confirmed?
A: Yes, based on cancellations.

Q: What is chart preparation?
A: Final seat allocation before departure.

Q: When is chart prepared?
A: Usually 4 hours before departure.

Q: Can I cancel my ticket?
A: Yes, through the IRCTC app or website.
